2. File Processing and Security

All files uploaded to Zip doc (including PDFs, Images, Word, PowerPoint, and Excel documents) are processed strictly for the purpose of performing the requested operation (compression, conversion, encryption, or unlocking).

  • Files are automatically purged and permanently deleted from our servers via automatic scheduled background cleanup scripts every 15 minutes.
